About & résumé

About me

I am a finance and accounting professional with nearly a decade of experience across audit, financial reporting, taxation and team leadership. I am currently an Assistant Manager in Accounts & Finance at As-Sunnah Foundation, where I review financial records, prepare financial statements, oversee bank reconciliations and coordinate external audits.

My career began with nearly four years of audit work at Rahman Mostafa Alam & Co., Chartered Accountants, which gave me a strong foundation in auditing standards and internal controls. Since then, I have worked across real estate, travel, international business (in Angola) and the non-profit sector.

Highlights include reducing month-end closing time from 5 to 3 days through reconciliation automation, leading a team of 7 finance professionals, and implementing a payroll system that eliminated processing errors.

I have completed the Professional Level of the ICAB Chartered Accountancy program and practise as an Income Tax Practitioner. I am comfortable with IFRS/IAS, VAT and income tax compliance, Tally ERP, QuickBooks and Excel.
